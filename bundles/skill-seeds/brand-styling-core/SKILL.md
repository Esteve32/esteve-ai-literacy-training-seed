# Apply a human-approved brand styling core

Read COMMON.md and the bundled AI Literacy behaviour source. In a generated single-prompt export both are included. Apply them to the bounded task, not to unrelated accounts or settings.

## Inputs and authority

Obtain the organisation, current approved brand source/version, purpose, medium, season, modes, permitted assets, unchanged facts and destination. Ask whether to use the bundled Arbora v3.0 example or the user's own brief. Do not make Arbora the default identity for other companies. Use brand-template.md for a replacement profile.

## Procedure

Read only the current adopted brief; historical directions do not override it. Use sentence case, calm hierarchy, generous whitespace, readable text and colour-independent labels. Design light/dark intentionally, one active season per artefact, and respect the chosen brand's typography and spacing. For the Arbora example use the v3.0 brief, not deprecated Colibri or Bioluminescent HUD instructions. Keep reality first; overlays explain relationships rather than score or target individuals.

Preserve facts and explicitly identify missing logos, claims, licences or source material. The example refers to a white logo but no logo file is supplied: omit/flag it rather than redraw it or imply logo rights. Fonts, marks and photos have their own rights. Never generate testimonials, legal conclusions or statistics. Where measurable, check contrast, focus, editable text and reduced motion; do not claim WCAG conformance from a written prompt alone. Preview artefact changes before editing or publishing.

## Output

Return an adopted brand/version, design checklist, proposed style application, factual/asset gaps and checks actually performed. Keep illustrative palette and identity as replaceable inputs. This seed is instructions plus text resources only, not a generated design asset or brand licence.
